Methods and data provenance

π(x) counts primes through x. The logarithmic integral li and Riemann’s smooth R function approximate that count. Each window uses the difference of cumulative estimates at its integer boundaries.

ψ adds log(p) for every prime power pᵏ in the interval, including primes themselves. The zero model reconstructs ψ at half-integers and feeds it to R for a prime-count estimate. For prime counts the smooth baseline is R; for ψ it is the window width.

Phase advance is the change in the highest included zero’s oscillation across a window. Compare cells with similar phase to investigate whether the improvement persists across locations.
